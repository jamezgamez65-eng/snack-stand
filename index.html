Import a spreadsheet

One row per ingredient an item uses, with these columns: Item, Ingredient, Amount used, Package price, Units per package, Unit, Store and optionally Selling price. Leave Item blank for an ingredient you only want to record. For something you sell straight from a pack (drinks, chips, cookies), leave Ingredient blank and put the pack price and count on the item's row.

In Google Sheets tap Share and set General access to Anyone with the link. The link is remembered, so next time you just tap “Update from spreadsheet”. Needs internet.
